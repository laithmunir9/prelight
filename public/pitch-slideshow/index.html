The problem

Knowing the answer is not the same as saying it under pressure.

student prepared

The moment: a student has a viva, pitch, interview, or presentation coming up.

What breaks: solo rehearsal never interrupts, challenges, distracts, or follows up.

The risk: the first realistic audience appears on the day that counts.

Make this specific, not generic. Sources: HI Bootcamp - Day 3.pdf pages 4-6; IEC Pitch Deck Template page 5.

What we built

Green Room turns solo rehearsal into a realistic audience.

1 Choose the room

Viva, pitch, interview, public speech, or casual discussion.

2 Speak out loud

The mic captures the turn, cleans the transcript, and tracks pace.

3 Get the room back

Personas ask, challenge, distract, encourage, and keep things moving.

The AI is not the pitch. The believable practice loop is the pitch.

Avoid the tech list. Sources: HI Bootcamp - Day 3.pdf pages 2 and 10; IEC pages 6-8.
